# Ultron v1.7.0

Ultron now recognises the live NPC standing beside the player, so BATTLE ME NOW no longer fails because an older saved route snapshot says he is elsewhere.

Player battles now exchange real prize money in both directions. Ultron also loses money when a trainer or wild battle blacks him out.

Gen 1 and Gen 2 guide knowledge now shapes honest preparation—before Whitney, for example, Ultron looks for a catch or trained teammate that can answer Miltank. Live registries and encounter tables supply mod-added species, learnsets, evolutions, grass, water and fishing encounters.

Optional adapters let Ultron use installed fishing, Bug-Catching Contest, Safari Zone, Wonder Trade and Mystery Gift systems. His campaign remains separate from player story flags, while real HM ownership and an active Pokemon that knows the move remain mandatory.
